Meesho · Internal tool · Built from scratch

AI query validation app

Problem
Our team spent most of a day by hand checking whether an AI analytics tool was writing correct SQL. The work was repetitive, and errors were easy to miss when tired.
Built
An app that runs a fixed set of test questions through the AI, compares its queries and their results against source-of-truth SQL, and flags only the mismatches for a person to review.

Meesho · AI product rollout · 5 business units

Getting an AI analytics tool adopted

Problem
We co-built a Databricks Genie tool that lets non-technical teams ask business and A/B-test questions in plain language. Adoption stalled at about 35%. The tool worked, but business users were used to passing their requirements to analysts instead of asking it themselves.
Did
Tackled the habit first: before each business unit's launch, we ran hands-on setup sessions where users tried the tool on their own questions, and added a live leaderboard ranking teams by adoption. Separately, I worked with our PM and Databricks to fix a 5,000-row export limit that made the tool unusable for teams needing larger datasets.
Result
Adoption rose to 80% across 5 business units, and analysts' ad-hoc SQL requests fell by half.

Meesho · Lending collections · Mixpanel, SQL

Repayment funnel and channel attribution

Problem
Collections reminders went out across WhatsApp, calls and field visits, but it wasn't clear which ones actually caused repayment.
Did
Mapped the repayment journey in Mixpanel, from message sent, delivered and read, to link opened, to payment. Built an attribution and A/B testing framework across 6M+ transactions, crediting WhatsApp only when payment followed a read and click within a set window.
Result
Repeat, low-risk borrowers paid on time regardless of reminders, while first-time borrowers dropped off most. We shifted budget and effort from users who'd pay anyway to high-risk segments.

In hindsight: I'd add a small holdout that gets no reminders, to measure each message's true incremental effect instead of relying on read-and-click attribution.
